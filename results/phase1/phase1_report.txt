                     GCNConv takes no edge features
  hyperparameters    graph models searched over 4 configurations

NOTES
  Tabular and graph models are scored on identical outer folds, so the
  comparison is paired.
  No graph model reached the baseline.
  Paired against the baseline on the same folds:
    GCN    mean difference +0.0918, baseline ahead on 5/5 folds, p = 0.012
    GAT    mean difference +0.0480, baseline ahead on 4/5 folds, p = 0.114
    GINE   mean difference +0.1711, baseline ahead on 5/5 folds, p = 0.003
  Five folds resolve only large differences; a p above ~0.05 means the models
  are not separated by this comparison, not that they perform equally.
  Weakest graph model: GINE.
  GINE carries the richest representation - bond features and an MLP
  aggregator - yet ranks last, so on 522 molecules the extra capacity
  costs more than the representation returns.
  Labels are literature-derived rather than assay-standardized; these models
  rank hypotheses rather than predict measured activity.
  The graph models were written and trained in July 2025 but their outputs were
  not retained, so the numbers above are a re-run under the Phase 0 protocol,
  not a recovery of the original ones.
